# die t

i find it amusing that the word "diet" contains the word "die" within it. as though to cultivate one's diet is to prepare oneself for death.

i am reminded of bonnie prince billy's powerful lyric, ["the older that we get, we know that nothing else for us is possible."](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Siqa0Ld2B0) yes. as we mature, we find more and more that nothing is possible, that nothingness is the only possibility, and therefore the source of all chance itself. therefore, to cultivate one's diet is to curtail all that stands in the way of becoming aligned with nothing, the source of all possibility.

certainly, eating healthy foods is one aspect of diet - which can be as simple as choosing to eat foods which minimize attachment and aid in cultivating mind-body harmony, for instance water-rich foods such as fruits and vegetables. but ultimately all foods are foods for thought, so we ought to be mindful of what we consume. you can eat anything you wish - but consuming nuts and bolts will be somewhat more taxing to the system, and is not optimal for living a functionally embodied life. similarly, any thought you can think is certainly entertainable, but not all thoughts are healthy or fit to sustain one who wishes to be aligned with a greater possibility for oneself.

the fact is, as energetic beings we live in a constant, buzzing buffet of thought food. often without realizing it, we go about the day collecting others' food for thought and mistaking it for our own. but please understand, we are not merely consumers but resonant conductors. the longer a thought is lingered upon consciously or not, the more we strength we give the thought to resonate with stronger and stronger ripples across the energetic field. whatever we focus on grows brighter, louder, more real. the closer and the longer you look at something, the more vivid and detailed it becomes. the sky is only as blue as one perceives it.

similarly, as one's deeper awareness of oneself grows, it inevitably increases in awareness of expanded possibilities for oneself to consider, in ways that are both harmful and helpful. in other words, the one who has ability to help most also possesses knowledge of that which is most harmful.

therefore, the practical advice is to consciously and care-fully choose the thoughtforms which you want to ring and sing in you. hunger only for what you wish to become. what you look at, listen to, touch, taste, smell are no replacement for the light within. but they can aid or hinder in one's ability to see it. so eat mindfully, ye walking dead.
