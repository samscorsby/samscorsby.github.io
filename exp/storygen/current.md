### the syruped hoof manifesto

a grin-lion found scorsby, who hummed "rrombo dombo di di dai!" scorsby smiled, offering his hoof: "my charter, my name!" the lion peered. *thump!* a cosmic kick. across the plains, a man's syrup-tree, birds included, soared to a pixel-city. the dazed lion pondered playing dead. but scorsby, a shimmering echo, was already nowhere.
