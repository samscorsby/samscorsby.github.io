this is the gleamingfold, where the glongingstons come from. it curves away from itself everywhere at once, so there is always more room than you would think.

every scorsby here is a different one, and it goes on like that forever. it is the same fold for everyone: the ones you meet were already here, and they will be here for whoever comes next.

drag to wander. tap someone to say hi.

(a hyperbolic plane, seven around each one. scorsby's face is made only of circles, and here circles stay circles.)
