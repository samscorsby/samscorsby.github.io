### marshlight

scorsby set glow-dot crabs loose in the moon-marsh. elastic smiles declared 'spirits!' joyous "rrombo dombo di di dai!" stardust paid; others zipped. slow mussel-mind raced pixel-hare. hare bolted; kin grinned at finish. bean-sprite, turnip-kin, bickering, then complimenting ("wobble holds sky!" "roots anchor all!"), shimmered in delight.
